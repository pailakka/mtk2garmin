# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Outdoor map users in Finland who need reliable offline topographic maps for Garmin devices, Android map apps, iPhone/iPad Cartograph, and BaseCamp. They may be hikers, hunters, dog handlers, landowners, boaters, forest workers, or technically capable hobbyists who want a practical map file that works away from network coverage.

## Product Purpose

Kartat.Hylly.org publishes free Finnish offline terrain-map downloads generated from open geospatial data. The site helps visitors choose the correct file for their device, understand what the map contains, and optionally support continued maintenance by buying installation instructions.

Success means users can quickly find the correct download, understand that the map files are free, and trust the support purchase as an honest way to fund hosting, data processing, and maintenance.

## Brand Personality

Practical, trustworthy, field-ready.

The voice is direct and concrete. It should feel like a careful engineer who uses the maps outdoors: clear about constraints, honest about what is free, and helpful without hype.

## Anti-references

Do not make the site feel like a generic SaaS landing page, a hard-sell ecommerce funnel, or a decorative outdoors brand with vague adventure imagery. Avoid hiding free downloads behind support messaging. Avoid glossy AI-style cards, over-soft shadows, repeated icon grids, fake urgency, inflated claims, and template-like section grammar.

## Design Principles

- Lead with utility: make the next useful action obvious.
- Sell support honestly: paid instructions fund the project, but downloads stay free.
- Build trust through specifics: name devices, file formats, data sources, dates, sizes, hashes, and limitations.
- Preserve field readability: design for mobile, sunlight, quick scanning, and older users.
- Prefer durable clarity over novelty: visual craft should make the site more credible, not more ornamental.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA for contrast, keyboard access, focus states, and readable text sizing. Support reduced-motion preferences. Keep Finnish copy plain and specific. Do not rely on color alone to communicate file choices, paid/free status, or compatibility.
